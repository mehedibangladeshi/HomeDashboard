# Home Management

The shared language for managing a household's spending, responsibilities, and schedule.

## Household and access

**Household**:
An isolated shared space containing members, financial records, chores, and calendar data.

**User Account**:
An authenticated identity belonging to one Household in the first release.
_Avoid_: Member, profile

**Membership**:
The relationship between a User Account and Household, including its assigned role.

**Owner**:
A household member with governance authority over privileged roles, ownership, export, and deletion.

**Manager**:
A household member responsible for daily financial, chore, calendar, and ordinary-member administration.

**Member**:
A household participant who can self-manage chores, manage the shared calendar, and submit spending for approval.

## Spending

**Household Ledger**:
The authoritative collection of approved and voided household financial records.

**Expense Submission**:
A Member's proposed spending record awaiting approval or rejection.

**Expense**:
Money actually spent and accepted into the Household Ledger.

**Purchase**:
An Expense representing one shopping event with one or more Purchase Items.

**Purchase Item**:
A named line within a Purchase, optionally classified independently from the Purchase.

**Category**:
A Household-defined classification used to organize spending and Budgets.

**Budget**:
A monthly, non-rolling spending limit for a Category.

**Obligation**:
A recurring expected payment such as rent, internet, or a subscription.
_Avoid_: Bill when referring to the recurring definition

**Due Occurrence**:
One dated instance generated from an Obligation.

**Payment Source**:
An optional Household-defined label describing how spending was paid, without representing a balance or bank connection.

## Responsibilities and schedule

**Chore**:
A one-time or recurring responsibility with one accountable assignee.

**Chore Occurrence**:
One dated instance of a Chore with its own completion state.

**Household Event**:
A standalone shared calendar entry not generated by a Chore or Obligation.

**Reminder**:
A scheduled notification associated with a Chore Occurrence, Due Occurrence, or Household Event.
